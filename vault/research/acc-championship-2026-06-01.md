## Analysis

### Key Themes

The 2025–2026 ACC Championship cycle was dominated by **Duke University across multiple sports**, making history as the first ACC school to capture football, men's basketball, and women's basketball championships in the same academic year.

**Football (December 2025):** Duke defeated Virginia 27–20 in overtime in a wild finish that knocked Virginia out of the College Football Playoff picture. The game went viral on YouTube with the overtime ending drawing massive engagement.

**Men's Basketball (March 2026):** Duke repeated as ACC Tournament champions, defeating No. 10 Virginia 74–70 at the Spectrum Center in Charlotte. The Boozer twins (Cameron & Cayden) were central to Duke's run, as were individual standout performances from Isaiah Evans.

**Baseball (May–June 2026):** Georgia Tech emerged as the surprise champion, defeating North Carolina 13–6 in the ACC Baseball Championship Game. Georgia Tech entered as the #1 seed and delivered a dominant performance. Multiple formats of the game are available on YouTube (full replay, highlights, trophy presentation).

**Other Sports:** Virginia showed strength in golf (back-to-back ACC Men's Golf Championship wins) and lacrosse (ACC Lacrosse Championship Final vs. North Carolina). The ACC Indoor Track & Field Championship also produced a dedicated playlist.

### Conflicting Perspectives

- Virginia's near-misses in both football (OT loss to Duke) and basketball (74–70 loss to Duke) make them the "bridesmaid" narrative of the year — strong programs that peaked just short of the title.
- North Carolina had a similar story in baseball, reaching the championship game but falling to Georgia Tech.
- Some analysts question Duke's football program sustainability at the top of the ACC, given the conference's increasing competition from SMU, Clemson, and others.

### Practical Takeaways

- **Duke is the ACC's dominant force in 2025–26** — any ACC championship preview should center on Duke's programs.
- **Georgia Tech baseball** is an emerging powerhouse worth tracking.
